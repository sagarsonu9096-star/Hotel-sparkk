Welcome

A simple, clean stay in the middle of Delhi

Hotel Sparkk Deluxe sits in Gali No. 8, Multani Dhanda, Paharganj, a short walk from New Delhi Railway Station and the main bazaar. Whether you are in Delhi for a night or a week, we keep things simple: a tidy room, fresh linen, a proper bed and a front desk that is happy to help.

Walk in through our lobby, check in at the reception and settle in. For rates and availability, message us on WhatsApp and we will reply with the best options for your dates.
